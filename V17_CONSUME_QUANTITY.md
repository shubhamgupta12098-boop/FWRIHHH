# FoodWise Pro v17 — Quantity-aware consumption

When the user taps ✓ Consumed on an inventory item, FoodWise now asks exactly how much was consumed.

- Numeric quantities such as 1 L, 500 g, 6 pcs, 2 packs support partial consumption.
- Quick buttons: 25%, 50%, All.
- Only the consumed portion moves to Consumed History.
- The remaining quantity stays in Inventory automatically.
- Cost and impact credit are split proportionally.
- Restore puts only that consumed quantity back into the matching inventory item.
- Consumed history visibly shows the quantity consumed and, for partial use, how much was left immediately after use.
