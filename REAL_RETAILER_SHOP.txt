FoodWise v10 - Real Retailer Shopping Hub

What is real in this build:
- Blinkit, Zepto, Flipkart Minutes, Swiggy Instamart and BigBasket open from FoodWise.
- A product can be searched on a chosen retailer.
- For a multi-item FoodWise cart, the list is copied to your clipboard and the selected retailer opens.
- Live price, stock, delivery address, login, OTP and payment stay on the retailer's official website/app.
- After you successfully place the external order, return to FoodWise and tap "Yes, add to inventory". The purchased items are then added to inventory and expiry tracking starts.

Why checkout is not silently placed on Blinkit/Zepto/Flipkart Minutes:
Those consumer services do not expose a general public checkout API that can simply be called with a personal API key. Never scrape private APIs or store retailer passwords/OTP/payment details.

Swiggy Instamart:
Swiggy publishes developer/MCP transactional tooling for approved/authenticated builders. True in-app Instamart ordering can be added later after you receive the required developer access and user-auth flow.

FoodWise local catalog prices are only estimates. The retailer is the source of truth for live prices and availability.
