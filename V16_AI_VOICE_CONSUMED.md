# FoodWise Pro v16

## Changes
- General-purpose Gemini Q&A: ask food, coding, study, writing, general knowledge, and everyday questions.
- FoodWise inventory context is applied only when relevant.
- Improved microphone permission handling and clearer voice errors.
- App voice commands still navigate the UI; unmatched speech is sent to AI.
- Dedicated Voice to AI button in Assistant.
- Inventory now keeps a Consumed history instead of deleting used items.
- Consumed items can be restored to inventory.

## Voice setup
Use Chrome/Edge on `http://localhost:3000`. Click the site icon next to the URL and allow Microphone.
